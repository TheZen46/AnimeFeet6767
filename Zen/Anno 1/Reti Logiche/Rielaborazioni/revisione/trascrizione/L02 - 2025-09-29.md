SH1 (contiene gli 1): $\bar{A}\bar{B}\bar{C} + \bar{A}\bar{B}C + A\bar{B}\bar{C} + AB\bar{C} = \bar{A}\bar{B}(\bar{C} + C) + A\bar{B}\bar{C} + AB\bar{C} = \bar{A}\bar{B} + A\bar{B}\bar{C} + AB\bar{C}$

SH2 (prende gli 0): $(A + \bar{B} + C) \cdot (A + \bar{B} + \bar{C}) \cdot (\bar{A} + B + \bar{C}) \cdot (\bar{A} + \bar{B} + \bar{C})$

Con $(A + \bar{B}) = $ pippo: $(\text{pippo} + C)(\text{pippo} + \bar{C}) = \text{pippo} + \text{pippo}\,\bar{C} + \text{pippo}\,C + C\bar{C} = \text{pippo}$, quindi $(A + \bar{B}) \cdot (\bar{A} + B + \bar{C}) \cdot (\bar{A} + \bar{B} + \bar{C})$.

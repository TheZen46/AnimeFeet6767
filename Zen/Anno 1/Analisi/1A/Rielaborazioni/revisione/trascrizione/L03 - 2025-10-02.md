[?] spiegato il fattoriale, messo nei coefficienti binomiali:

$$\binom{n}{k} = \frac{n!}{k!\,(n-k)!} \quad\longrightarrow\quad = \binom{n-1}{k-1} + \binom{n-1}{k}$$

$$\frac{(n-1)!}{(k-1)!\,(n-1-k+1)!} + \frac{(n-1)!}{k!\,(n-1-k)!}$$

Abbiamo fatto la somma:

$$\frac{(n-1)!}{(k-1)!\,(n-k)!} + \frac{(n-1)!}{k\,(k-1)!\,(n-k-1)!} \;\longrightarrow\; \frac{(n-1)!}{(k-1)!\,(n-k-1)!} \cdot \left(\frac{1}{n-k} + \frac{1}{k}\right)$$

$$= \frac{(n-1)!}{(k-1)!\,(n-k-1)!} \cdot \frac{n}{k\,(n-k)} = \frac{n!}{(n-k)!\,k!}$$

A lato:

$$\frac{n!}{k!\,(n-k)!} = \frac{n!}{(n-k)!\,\big(n-(n-k)\big)!} = \frac{n!}{k!\,(n-k)!}$$

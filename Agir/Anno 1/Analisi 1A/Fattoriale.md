$n! = n\cdot n-1\cdot \ldots \cdot 1$

$0! = 1$ per convenzione

${n\choose{K}} = \frac{n!}{K!(n-K)!}$

${5\choose{3}} = \frac{5!}{3!(5-3)!} = \frac{5!}{3!\cdot2!} = \frac{5\cdot4\cdot3!}{3!\cdot2!}$

${n\choose{K}} = {{n-1}\choose{K-1}}+{{n-1}\choose{K}}$

$\frac{n!}{K!(n-K)!}=\frac{(n-1)!}{(K-1)!(n-K)!}+\frac{(n-1)!}{K!(n-1-K)!}=$

$\frac{(n-1)!}{(K-1)!(n-K-1)!}(\frac{1}{n-k}+\frac{1}{k})$

$\frac{(n-1)!}{(K-1)!(n-K-1)!}\cdot\frac{n}{K(n-K)}$

${6\choose{3}}={5\choose{2}}+{5\choose{3}}$

$\frac{6!}{3!(6-3)!}={\frac{5!}{2!(5-2)!}+{\frac{5}{3!(5-3)!}}}=\frac{5!}{2!3!}+\frac{5!}{3!2!}=\frac{2\cdot5!}{3!2!}=\frac{5!}{3!}=\frac{5\cdot4\cdot3!}{3!}=20$

$\frac{6\cdot5\cdot4\cdot3!}{3!(6-3)!}=\frac{6\cdot5\cdot4\cdot}{3!}=\frac{120}{6}=20$

${n\choose{K}} = {n\choose{n-K}} = \frac{n!}{K!(n-K)!} = \frac{n!}{(n-K)!(n-n+K)} = \frac{n!}{(n-K)!K!}$

$\frac{(n+3)!}{n!} = \frac{(n+3)(n+2)(n+1)n!}{n!} = (n+3)(n+2)(n+1)$

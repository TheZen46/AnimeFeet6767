# Retta dei minimi quadrati
$y=\frac{\delta y}{\delta x}\rho_{x,y}(x-\overline x)+\overline y$
$\overline x$ media delle $x_1,\ldots,x_n$
$\overline y$ media delle $y_1,\ldots,y_n$
$\delta_x$ deviazione standard
$\delta_y$ deviazione standard
$\rho_{x,y}$ coefficiente di correlazione

$\overline x=\frac75$
$\overline y=-\frac1{10}$
$\delta_x=\frac{\sqrt6}5$
$\delta_y=\frac{\sqrt{69}}{10}$
$\rho_{x,y}=-\frac{13}{3\sqrt{46}}$

$m^\star=\frac{\delta y}{\delta x}\rho_{x,y}=\frac{\frac{\sqrt{69}}{10}}{\frac{\sqrt6}5}\left(-\frac{13}{3\sqrt{46}}\right)=-\frac{13}{12}\approx-1.08$

$y=-\frac{13}{12}(x-\frac75)-\frac1{10}=-\frac{13}{12}x+\frac{17}{12}\approx1.4$

$y=0$ quando $-\frac{13}{12}x+\frac{17}{12}=0\rightarrow x=\frac{17}{13}\approx1.3$


$x=0\qquad\hat y=\left(m^\star x+q^\star\right)_{x=0}=\left(-\frac{13}{12}x+\frac{17}{12}\right)_{x=0}=\frac{17}{12}$
$x=3\qquad\hat y=\left(m^\star x+q^\star\right)_{x=3}=\left(-\frac{13}{12}x+\frac{17}{12}\right)_{x=3}=\frac{-39+17}{12}=\frac{-22}{12}=-\frac{11}6$
